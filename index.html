One hue, one way to the sea
Everything that reaches the sea by the same mouth shares a hue. Neighbouring basins are given hues well apart; the hue itself carries no other meaning, except that blue is kept for water.

On a card, / is a confluence and > the same river under a new name, read from the mouth up: Thames / Cherwell / Ray.
The main stem runs dark, its tributaries light, and each tributary valley is split the same way again. Zoom in and the valleys divide further, five times over. Basins with no way to the sea are drawn muted.
